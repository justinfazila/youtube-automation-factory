# Logo & Branding Assets

## Logo Specifications
- **Format**: SVG (vector) + PNG (512x512, 1024x1024)
- **Colors**: 
  - Primary: #FFD700 (Gold/Yellow)
  - Secondary: #1A1A2E (Dark Navy)
  - Accent: #FF0000 (Red)
- **Typography**: Montserrat Black / Anton
- **Style**: Minimalist, tech-inspired, recognizable at small sizes

## Thumbnail Templates
- **Format**: 1280x720 PNG/JPG
- **Template Structure**:
  - Left: Character/Visual element (60% width)
  - Right: Text overlay (40% width) - Max 3-4 words
  - Background: Dark gradient with subtle pattern

## Channel Banner
- **Format**: 2560x1440 PNG
- **Safe Area**: 1546x423 (center)
- **Content**: Channel name, tagline, upload schedule
